### PM data (`src/data/`)
- **`pm_markets.py`**: metadata (archive + 24/09 export), event and series keys, broad macro pre-filter. We keep 49.6k Polymarket markets (7.6k events) and 75k Kalshi markets (11.6k events).
- **`pm_panel.py`**: daily panel at the 16:00 London snapshot, with daylight saving handled date by date.
  - Content: last price, VWAP of the last hour, volume, and price at least 4 h before the snapshot (for the false-shock filter).
  - Size: 30k Polymarket markets (USD 8.9bn of volume) and 50k Kalshi markets (USD 2.3bn), in 2 minutes of computation.
- **Two data problems found and fixed**:
  - `amount_usd` is wrong (often negative). Volume is recomputed as |price × size| (D10).
  - The live tape is nearly empty after 24/09. The sample therefore ends on **2026-09-24** (D9).
- **Continuity checked** at the archive / live junction of 21/07.

### Rule-based classification (`src/pm/classify.py`, no LLM)
- **Reused components**:
  - Arb's `geo_registry` for countries, central bankers and leaders;
  - kairos-main's `classifier_v2`;
  - a hand-written table of Kalshi series.
- **Directions**: regex per theme. For elections, `data/manual/party_fiscal_stance.csv` is used (sources in comments).
- **Partitions** recognised and converted to a common unit:
  - Fed and ECB decisions in bp;
  - number of rate cuts converted to bp;
  - inflation brackets in pp;
  - tariff rates in %.
- **First audit** on the most liquid events: 8 defects fixed (e.g. "by end of 2026" read as a de-escalation).
- **31 rule tests pass.**
